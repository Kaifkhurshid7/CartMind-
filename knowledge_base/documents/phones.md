# Phone buying guide

## RAM and storage
8GB RAM is comfortable for most users; 12GB helps heavy multitasking and gaming. 128GB storage is enough if you use cloud photos, while 256GB suits those who shoot lots of video. UFS 3.1 and 4.0 storage is faster than eMMC.

## Camera
Megapixel count is not the whole story. Sensor size, lens quality and image processing decide quality. A 50MP sensor with optical stabilisation often beats a 200MP sensor on a poor lens in low light.

## Battery and charging
5000mAh is the modern baseline and 6000mAh lasts about two days of normal use. 67W or 80W fast charging fills a phone in under an hour.

## Display
AMOLED panels have better contrast than LCD. 120Hz refresh rate makes scrolling smoother but uses a little more battery.
