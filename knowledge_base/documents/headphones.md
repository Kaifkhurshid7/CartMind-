# Headphones buying guide

## Active noise cancellation (ANC)
ANC uses microphones and inverse sound waves to cancel steady low-frequency noise such as aircraft engines, fans and traffic hum. It is less effective against voices and sudden sounds. Hybrid ANC with feed-forward and feed-back microphones works best. Over-ear headphones usually cancel more noise than budget earbuds.

## Battery life
Over-ear wireless headphones typically give 30-60 hours without ANC. Turning ANC on reduces battery life by about 10-20%. Fast charging that gives several hours from a 10-minute charge is useful.

## Types
Over-ear headphones are comfortable for long sessions and have larger drivers. In-ear buds are portable and good for the gym. Neckbands have long battery life but no strong ANC.

## Sound
Bass-heavy tuning is common in budget headphones. Codecs such as AAC, LDAC and aptX improve wireless quality on supported phones. Bluetooth 5.3 gives better stability and lower power use than 5.0.
